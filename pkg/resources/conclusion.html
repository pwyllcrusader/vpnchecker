vpnchecker is running

It starts with the Mac and needs no window. While the VPN is down, Claude, Cursor and Rider are paused; they continue by themselves a moment after it is back.

In Terminal:

vpnchecker statuswhat it sees and does right now
vpnchecker allow example.comlet a site work without the VPN
vpnchecker logs -fthe service log
sudo vpnchecker stopswitch it off and release everything
sudo vpnchecker uninstallremove it

Settings are in ~/.config/vpnchecker/config.yaml; apply a change with vpnchecker reload.

When a new version appears, vpnchecker asks before installing it.
