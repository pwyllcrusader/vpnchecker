vpnchecker @VERSION@

A VPN kill switch: Claude, Cursor and Rider reach the internet only through the VPN. If you want, the whole Mac is restricted the same way.

Connect your VPN before you continue. vpnchecker starts only once it sees the VPN. Otherwise it would cut the connection right away.

After you enter your Mac password, setup asks one question: what to restrict while the VPN is down, the whole Mac or only Claude, Cursor and Rider.

Then it installs:

  • the background service, /Library/PrivilegedHelperTools/vpnchecker
  • the vpnchecker command, /usr/local/bin/vpnchecker
  • your settings, ~/.config/vpnchecker/config.yaml (an existing file is kept)

If vpnchecker is already installed, this updates it and asks nothing.
